Saved captures from the dashboard, photographed on 6 October. They stay here whether or not the camera is running.

How to read it

  • BannerGO in green, STOP in red, NO PERSON in grey. NO SIGNAL means this page cannot reach the sentry, so it never keeps showing an old GO.
  • Camera viewThe sentry’s own frame, with the verdict drawn in the corner.
  • MessageWhat the sentry says to the person. Blank when nobody is in frame.
  • ReadoutThe model backend, the people counted, how long the model took on this frame, and how old the frame is. Frame age turns amber past 2.5 seconds.

Served from the Jetson next to the sentry, and it works without internet. Add ?backend=vlm_ollama_ministral to the address to pick a backend when the sentry is not locked to one. Add ?kiosk=1 for the full-screen view made for a small LCD. The three reference photos were taken of a monitor, so colour and sharpness are not the dashboard’s own.
